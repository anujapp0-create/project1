Frequently asked questions

What is a B2B tax invoice?
An invoice issued to another GST-registered business, showing both parties’ GSTINs and the correct tax breakup.
When do I need an E-Way bill?
Generally for movement of goods worth more than ₹50,000. Enter the number in the E-Way bill field.
How is CGST/SGST vs IGST decided?
By place of supply — same state as you means CGST + SGST; a different state means IGST.
Do large B2B invoices need an IRN?
Yes — suppliers above the e-invoicing turnover limit must generate an IRN + QR from the government portal for B2B invoices.
