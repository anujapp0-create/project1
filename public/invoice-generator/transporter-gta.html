Invoicing as a Goods Transport Agency (GTA)

For many GTA services, GST is paid by the recipient under reverse charge (RCM). This generator pre-sets SAC 9965 and the RCM declaration, so your invoice correctly states that tax is payable by the recipient rather than charging it yourself.

If you’ve opted to pay GST under forward charge, turn off the RCM toggle and select your rate instead.

GTA tip: Under RCM, you don’t collect GST — the recipient pays it. The invoice must clearly declare this. If you’ve opted for forward charge (12% with ITC / 5% without), switch off RCM and set the rate.

Frequently asked questions

What SAC code do transporters use?
Goods transport by road by a GTA is SAC 9965.
Who pays GST on GTA services?
In many cases the recipient pays under reverse charge (RCM). Some GTAs opt for forward charge instead.
How do I show reverse charge on the invoice?
The RCM toggle is on by default here — it sets tax to 0 on your invoice and adds a declaration that GST is payable by the recipient.
What if I charge GST myself (forward charge)?
Turn off the RCM toggle and pick your rate (commonly 12% with ITC or 5% without).
