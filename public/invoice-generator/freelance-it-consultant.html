Invoicing as a freelancer or IT consultant

Independent professionals and IT consultants usually supply services under SAC 9983 (or a related 998x code) at 18% GST. This generator pre-fills those defaults and an hourly unit, so you can bill by the hour or by project in a couple of clicks.

If your client is in another state, it automatically switches to IGST; if they’re in your state, it splits into CGST + SGST.

Common SAC codes for services

  • 9983 — Other professional, technical and business services
  • 998314 — IT design & development services
  • 9982 — Legal and accounting services
Tip for freelancers: If you’re under the GST threshold and unregistered, you don’t charge GST — leave GSTIN blank and set the rate to 0%. Once registered, use your SAC and the correct rate.

Frequently asked questions

What SAC code do IT consultants use?
IT and software consulting is commonly SAC 9983 / 998314 at 18%. Confirm the exact sub-code for your service.
How do I bill by the hour?
The unit is pre-set to Hours — enter your hourly rate and the number of hours as the quantity.
Do freelancers need to charge GST?
Only if you are registered under GST (generally after crossing the turnover threshold). Unregistered freelancers don’t charge GST.
Is the invoice valid for my clients?
It includes the standard GST tax-invoice fields. For large clients above the e-invoicing limit, a government IRN may also be required.
