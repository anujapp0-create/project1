Billing for manpower supply services

Manpower and staffing agencies supply services under SAC 9985 at 18% GST. This generator pre-fills two lines — reimbursement of staff wages and your agency service charge — so your invoice separates the two clearly, which clients and auditors prefer.

GST generally applies on the full invoice value for a manpower supply (not only the service charge) unless you qualify as a pure agent — check your contract.

Manpower tip: Unless you strictly meet the "pure agent" conditions, GST is charged on the total (wages + service charge), not just your margin. Structure the lines accordingly.

Frequently asked questions

What SAC code applies to manpower supply?
Manpower/staffing supply is SAC 9985 at 18% GST.
Is GST charged on wages too?
Generally yes — on the full value of a manpower supply, unless you strictly qualify as a pure agent for the wage portion.
Can I show wages and service charge separately?
Yes — this template pre-fills two lines so both are itemised on the invoice.
Does the tax split change by state?
Yes — intra-state is CGST + SGST, inter-state is IGST, based on the place of supply.
