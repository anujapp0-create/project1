About GSTInvoice2GSTR1

We turn the tedious part of GST filing — typing invoices one by one — into a few clicks.

GSTInvoice2GSTR1 was built for Indian Chartered Accountants, GST practitioners, and businesses who spend hours every month keying sales invoices into spreadsheets and accounting software. Upload your invoice PDFs or photos, and the tool reads each one, sorts them into the right GSTR-1 tables, builds the HSN and document summaries, and hands you a filing-ready Excel.

What we believe

  • Your time is better spent on review, not data entry. The tool does the typing; you do the checking.
  • You stay in control. Every extracted field is shown for review and editing before anything is downloaded.
  • Accuracy matters. The registered person is responsible for the return, so we make verification quick and clear.

Who runs it

GSTInvoice2GSTR1 is operated by TaxTrack Solutions, based in Faridabad, Haryana, India. We come from a tax and advisory background and build practical tools for compliance professionals.
