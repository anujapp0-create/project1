Frequently asked questions

What file types can I upload?
GST sales-invoice PDFs and photos/screenshots (JPG, PNG), including invoices clients share on WhatsApp.
Is the extracted data always accurate?
It's usually very accurate, but not guaranteed — especially on blurry photos. That's why every field is shown in an editable review table before download. Always verify GSTIN, place of supply, rate and values before filing.
What do I get as output?
A GSTR-1 ready Excel working with B2B, B2CL, B2CS, exports, credit/debit notes, HSN summaries and the document summary.
How does pricing work?
New accounts get 10 free pages. After that it's ₹249 per 100 page credits, prepaid. You're only charged for pages we successfully read.
Do I need an account?
Yes — a quick Google sign-in. Your page credits are saved to your account, so they work across devices.
Is my invoice data safe?
Uploaded files are processed and not stored on our servers after your conversion completes. See our Privacy Policy.
Can the output go into the GST Offline Utility?
Yes — the Excel follows the GSTR-1 table structure, so you can review it and use it with the GST Offline Utility. Always verify the data before filing.
Can I get a refund?
Failed conversions are never charged. Unused credits can be refunded within the window described in our Refund Policy.
